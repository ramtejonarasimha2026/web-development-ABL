DOCTORS DEPARTMENTS HOME
  1. Emergency & Critical Care:
    • Emergency Department (ED / ER / Casualty): Provides immediate medical and surgical care for acute trauma, accidents, and life-threatening conditions.
    • Intensive Care Unit (ICU): Specialized monitoring and advanced life support for critically ill patients (includes sub-units like NICU for neonates, PICU for children, and CCU for coronary care).
    • Trauma Care: Dedicated facility for severe physical injuries and resuscitation.
    2.
  2. General & Internal Medicine
    • General Medicine / Internal Medicine: Diagnosis and non-surgical management of common, systemic, and chronic adult illnesses.
    • Cardiology: Diagnosis and treatment of heart and cardiovascular disorders.
    • Neurology: Medical treatment for disorders of the brain, spinal cord, and nerves (e.g., strokes, epilepsy).
    • Gastroenterology: Care for conditions affecting the stomach, liver, pancreas, and digestive tract.
    • Pulmonology / Respiratory Medicine: Treatment of lung diseases such as asthma, COPD, and pneumonia.
    • Nephrology: Kidney diseases and dialysis support.
    • Endocrinology: Hormonal and metabolic conditions (e.g., diabetes, thyroid disorders).
    • Dermatology: Skin, hair, and nail disorders.
    • Rheumatology: Autoimmune disorders, arthritis, and joint diseases.
    3.
  3. Surgical Specialties
    • General Surgery: Surgical treatment for abdominal issues, hernias, appendix removals, and soft-tissue procedures.
    • Orthopedics: Treatment of bones, joints, ligaments, tendons, and fractures.
    • Neurosurgery: Operative procedures for the brain, skull, and spine.
    • Cardiothoracic Surgery: Open-heart surgeries, bypass grafting, and lung operations.
    • Urology: Surgical treatment of the urinary tract and male reproductive system.
    • Otolaryngology (ENT): Ear, nose, throat, head, and neck surgeries.
    • Ophthalmology: Eye surgery and vision care.
    • Plastic & Reconstructive Surgery: Cosmetic procedures and reconstruction after burns or trauma.
    4.
  4. Maternal & Child Health
    • Obstetrics & Gynecology (OB-GYN): Pregnancy, childbirth, and female reproductive health.
    • Pediatrics: Medical care and routine vaccinations for infants, children, and adolescents.
    5.
  5. Oncology & Specialized Care
    • Oncology: Cancer treatment, broken down into:
    • Medical Oncology (Chemotherapy, immunotherapy)
    • Surgical Oncology (Tumor resection)
    • Radiation Oncology (Radiation therapy)
